Work with us

Have a project that needs technology behind it? We would like to hear about it.

What we look for

Ideas and projects in any sector where technology (hardware, software or automation) can make a real difference. Outside that, we look at each case on its own merits.

What we can offer

We explore partnerships case by case, which may include funding and equity participation, technical development, and access to suppliers and production.

How it works

Send us a short summary. We read every message and, when there is a fit, we talk about the best way to work together: funding, development, partnership or participation.

Contact

Please do not send confidential or proprietary information in your first message. Sending an idea does not create any obligation or rights for either side, and we cannot guarantee a reply.
